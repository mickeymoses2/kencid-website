# KENCID Course Image Generation System

Use this master prompt for every course image across the website. Only change
the course name, school, practical activity, environment, and people. Every
course must receive a distinct image file; never reuse an image between
courses.

## Master prompt

Create a premium photorealistic 16:9 course image for the KENCID website.

Keep this brand language consistent across every image:

- Brand yellow: `#FCD11E`
- Pink: `#E84880`
- Blue: `#48B8F8`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Black may be used as a supporting neutral.

Integrate the brand colours naturally into real objects in the scene: clothing,
furniture, books, equipment, fabric, artwork, stationery, chairs, studio
props, screens, tools, or décor. Do not add artificial colour overlays,
gradients, floating graphics, decorative shapes, or poster treatments over the
photograph.

Show believable Black African / Kenyan campus-age students or young
professionals actively learning and making. Use a modern, beautifully designed
learning environment with depth, layered backgrounds, premium lighting,
realistic materials, and a slightly cinematic editorial look. Subjects should
be natural, confident, approachable, and engaged in the course activity rather
than posing like generic stock photography.

Use professional photography characteristics: high dynamic range, realistic
skin and hands, crisp subject focus, detailed textures, soft background
separation, natural depth of field, balanced highlights and shadows, and
premium commercial photography quality.

Compose the image for both a course card and a course-detail hero: horizontal
16:9, a clear course-relevant focal point, balanced visual weight, and safe
cropping at desktop and mobile widths. The subject does not need to be placed
on the right and the frame does not need left-side headline space; this is a
course image, not a marketing hero background. Make every course composition
meaningfully different from the others.

Do not place course names, headlines, captions, buttons, readable labels,
logos, or marketing text inside the image. Avoid overly dark scenes, excessive
pink, oversaturation, fake neon, cartoon styling, 3D renders, vector art,
graphic-design posters, collages, unrealistic architecture, distorted hands,
duplicated people, obvious AI artefacts, watermarks, and generic corporate
stock-photo scenes.
